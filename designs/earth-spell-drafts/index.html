Updated Golem rule

  • 20+ Shield transforms Earth. Shield loss during a turn leaves the Golem active until Earth’s own end-turn check.
  • At Earth’s end turn, check Shield first. Without protection, 19 or lower reverts the Golem and prevents its automatic damage.
  • If Golem form remains active, it deals 15 damage to the opponent. This replaces the previous +10 spell-damage bonus.
  • Earth-4 protects an already-active Golem through the next Earth turn. Protection expires for the normal Shield check at that turn’s end.
