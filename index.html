THE WIZARD’S HANDBOOK

Fire meets Water

Share one screen locally, or choose Play on two screens to create or join a hosted room. Each wizard has their own hand, spell tower, and discard pile.

  • Start with 30 HP, 3 mana, and 4 spell slabs. Each deck has 30 slabs, leaving 26 in the opening tower. Shield, Embers/Flow, and loaded dice start at zero.
  • Each wizard rolls one opening die. The higher roll takes the first turn; ties make both wizards roll again. Opening rolls use no Fate or Loaded bonuses.
  • The wizard going second draws one extra slab at the beginning of their first turn, normally starting that turn with 5 rather than 4. Slabs used for defending Fate before that turn remain spent. This opening draw happens once and grants no extra mana.
  • Both wizards take one turn per round. Each gains 2 mana at the start of their own turn after their opening turn. Unspent mana carries over.
  • After each wizard’s opening turn, draw up to 5 slabs at the beginning of their turn.
  • Each player may cast one primary spell per turn. Elemental and Neutral slabs are primary spells. Cast Spell pays the printed mana cost; it stays disabled after that spell resolves. A spell without a roll condition resolves immediately. Ember spending choices are made before casting.
  • A spell with a roll threshold opens Fate. Select any number of eligible Fate slabs, then Cast Fate plays the whole batch and advances immediately. Skip Fate advances without playing selected slabs. Fate does not count as another primary spell.
  • The attacker may use Fortune (+4) and Twist (+2). Their Cast Fate or Skip Fate passes to the defender, who may use Misfortune (−4) and Twist (−2). The defender’s Cast Fate or Skip Fate advances to the roll. Add both sides’ Fate and the caster’s eligible Loaded bonus to two dice.
  • Incoming damage, including self-damage, consumes Shield before HP. Healing stops at 30 HP. Reaching 0 HP ends the duel.
  • Fire Embers are capped at 5. Only printed spell effects grant Embers; casting a Fire slab does not grant an automatic Ember. Spending removes the chosen Embers before resolution.
  • Water spells use their existing Flow for effects and damage. After resolving, gain 1 Flow, or the printed Flow gain instead. These gains do not stack. Flow is capped at 5; Neutral and Fate slabs do not grant the automatic gain.
  • A Loaded bonus earned this turn is queued for your next own turn. It applies once to your first roll in that turn; if unused, it expires when that turn ends.
  • End Turn turns red and reveals Hand Refresh. Click the red End Turn to pass without refreshing.
  • Click Hand Refresh to turn it red and select any number of slabs (including zero). Click it again to discard those selections, draw up to five, and pass immediately. Or click the red End Turn to pass without applying the refresh selections.
Playtest Fate sequence: attacker → defender → roll. Shared-screen games use handoffs. Two-screen games let each player respond on their own device; the server rolls and resolves after both Fate windows finish. Next-turn Loaded bonuses do not affect spells cast during the turn they were earned.

Build decks from Home: exactly 30 slabs, up to 3 copies per spell, and one main element plus Neutral and Fate. There are no category quotas. Rules text and mana costs come from the uploaded master spellbook. Select a slab and choose Read slab for full text. Click Spell Tower to review this battle’s actual composition.
