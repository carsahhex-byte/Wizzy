THE WIZARD’S HANDBOOK

Fire meets Water

A duel for two players sharing a screen, using your original pixel art. Each wizard has their own hand, spell tower, and discard pile.

  • Start with 30 HP, 3 mana, and 4 spell slabs. Each deck has 30 slabs, leaving 26 in the opening tower. Shield, Embers/Flow, and loaded dice start at zero.
  • Both wizards take one turn per round. At the beginning of every round after the first, both gain 2 mana. Unspent mana carries over.
  • After each wizard’s opening turn, draw up to 5 slabs at the beginning of their turn.
  • Select a slab, then click Cast Spell. The button becomes Skip Fate and all hand slabs are greyed out.
  • Click Skip Fate to resolve the spell. After resolution, the slab moves to discard and End Turn becomes available again. You may cast another spell before ending.
  • End Turn reveals Hand Refresh. To skip refreshing, click End Turn again to pass.
  • Click Hand Refresh, select any number of slabs (including zero), then click Hand Refresh again. Selected slabs are greyed out, discarded, and replaced by drawing up to five. Click End Turn to pass. Refresh is available once per turn.
Spell slabs are connected to the supplied spellbook. Names, mana costs, and rules text are displayed as reference. Casting does not yet spend mana or execute effects. Fate timing, dice, damage, and other effect rules will be implemented next; all slabs still grey out during the fate window.

The Fire v2.2 and Water v1.0 lists each contain 21 elemental, 2 Neutral, and 7 Fate slabs. Fire and Water use their elemental artwork; Neutral slabs use white and Fate slabs use purple. Select a slab and choose Read slab to inspect its full text. Click Spell Tower to review quantities, without revealing draw order. Older sample decks upgrade to 30 slabs while preserving duel progress.
