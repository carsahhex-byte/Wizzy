THE WIZARD’S HANDBOOK

Fire meets Water

A duel for two players sharing a screen, using your original pixel art. Each wizard has their own hand, spell tower, and discard pile.

  • Start with 30 HP, 3 mana, and 4 cards. Shield, Embers/Flow, and loaded dice start at zero.
  • Both wizards take one turn per round. At the beginning of every round after the first, both gain 2 mana. Unspent mana carries over.
  • After each wizard’s opening turn, draw up to 5 cards at the beginning of their turn.
  • Select a card, then click Cast Spell. The button becomes Skip Fate and all hand cards are greyed out.
  • Click Skip Fate to resolve the spell. After resolution, the card moves to discard and End Turn becomes available again. You may cast another spell before ending.
  • End Turn reveals Hand Refresh. To skip refreshing, click End Turn again to pass.
  • Click Hand Refresh, select any number of cards (including zero), then click Hand Refresh again. Selected cards are greyed out, discarded, and replaced by drawing up to five. Click End Turn to pass. Refresh is available once per turn.
Card effects, fate cards, and dice rules are coming next. No costs, damage, or random dice outcomes are invented. Skip Fate currently resolves the card flow only. Dice display the supplied artwork.

Each wizard has a temporary 24-card deck: Fiery Comet for Fire and Cresting Wave for Water. If a tower runs out, drawing stops. Browser saves resume your current phase; older duels are upgraded to Water automatically.
