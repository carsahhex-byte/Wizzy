THE WIZARD’S HANDBOOK

Fire meets Water

Share one screen locally, or choose Play on two screens to create or join a hosted room. Each wizard has their own hand, spell tower, and discard pile.

  • Start with 100 HP, 3 Mana, and 4 spell slabs. Caps are 100 HP, 10 Mana, and 30 Shield. Each saved deck has 30 slabs, leaving 26 in the opening tower. Shield, Embers/Flow, and Loaded start at zero.
  • Each wizard rolls one opening die. The higher roll takes the first turn; ties make both wizards roll again. Opening rolls use no Fate or Loaded bonuses.
  • The wizard going second draws one extra slab at the beginning of their first turn, normally starting that turn with 5 rather than 4. Slabs used for defending Fate before that turn remain spent. This opening draw happens once and grants no extra mana.
  • Both wizards take one turn per round. Each gains 2 Mana at the start of their own turn after their opening turn. Unspent Mana carries over up to 10.
  • After each wizard’s opening turn, draw up to 5 slabs at the beginning of their turn.
  • Each player may cast one primary spell per turn. Fire and Water slabs are primary spells. Cast Spell pays the printed Mana cost; it stays disabled after the spell resolves. Ember spending choices are made before casting. Fire-7 and Fire-14 spend all Embers automatically.
  • All current primary spells roll 2d6 before Fate. The dice and eligible Loaded bonus are displayed first. Select any number of eligible Fate slabs, then Cast Fate plays the whole batch. Skip Fate advances without playing staged slabs. Fate does not count as another primary spell.
  • The defender decides first, followed by the caster. A caster skip resolves the spell. If the caster plays Fate, the defender gets one final response, then the spell resolves. A player with no eligible Fate is skipped automatically. Fortune gives the caster +4; Misfortune gives the defender −4. Either player may choose +2 or −2 for each Twist. Fate changes the Modified Roll without rerolling the dice.
  • Opponent damage consumes Shield before HP. Self-damage bypasses your Shield. Healing stops at 100 HP, and Shield gains stop at 30. Reaching 0 HP ends the duel.
  • Fire Embers are capped at 5. Only printed spell effects grant Embers; casting a Fire slab does not grant an automatic Ember. Spending removes the chosen Embers before resolution.
  • Water spells use their existing Flow for effects, damage, and healing. After resolving, gain 1 Flow; a printed additional Flow adds to this gain. Flow is capped at 5. Fate slabs grant no automatic Flow.
  • A Loaded bonus earned this turn is queued for your next own turn. It applies once to your first roll in that turn; if unused, it expires when that turn ends.
  • End Turn turns red and reveals Hand Refresh. Click the red End Turn to pass without refreshing.
  • Click Hand Refresh to turn it red and select any number of slabs (including zero). Click it again to discard those selections, draw up to five, and pass immediately. Or click the red End Turn to pass without applying the refresh selections.
Fate sequence: roll → defender → caster → final defender response if the caster played Fate. Shared-screen games use handoffs. Two-screen games let each player respond on their own device. Empty eligible Fate hands are skipped automatically. Next-turn Loaded bonuses do not affect the spell that earned them.

Build decks from Home: exactly 30 slabs, up to 3 copies per spell, and Fire or Water plus Fate. New deck slots start empty. Upload deck loads a JSON draft for review; Save deck makes it playable, and Download deck exports a complete deck. The November 4 spellbook supplies all 31 spell definitions. Fate retains its previous 0 Mana cost because the PDF leaves it unspecified. Select a slab and choose Read slab for full text. Click Spell Tower to review this battle’s composition.
